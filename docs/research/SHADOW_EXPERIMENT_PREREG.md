## 1. Hypotheses and decision rule (unchanged from the design)
S1 (informative floor), S2 (at least 3 unique catches in at least 3 repositories over arms 1 and 3), S3 (lenient policy: review burden at most 30% and false-clear at most 25% of positives; strict reported), S4 (text triage versus arm 5 on non-Python). **Decision rule for an acting consumer:** it may be built (as engineering) only if S1 and S2 hold and the false-clear rate under the policy the owner would run is at most 25%; otherwise it is not built. Either outcome is reported with denominators. If fewer than 20 positives are found, the study reports mechanics only and does not apply the decision rule.

## 2. Pool
`python3 research/shadow2/pool.py --seed 1606796364189339605 --out data/shadow2/pool.json` (defaults as in section 0). Pull requests whose repository or pull request is no longer observable are recorded as such, not replaced.

## 3. Arms (computed for every pool pull request before any label exists)
- **0 Random**: seeded hash ordering.
- **1 CI only**: the head commit's check-runs and status. Ranking: failing or pending (2) > no CI (1) > success (0); binary decision: proceed only on success.
- **2 Trust the report**: review unless the description reports passing tests or a clean lint (a `tests_pass` or `lint_clean` claim).
- **3 Text triage**: the count of distinct phrases from a fixed list of confident-closing phrases (`cheap_arms.py`); ties by seeded hash. (A TF-IDF logistic baseline trained on the audit-study labels is an additional, clearly labelled arm 3b run only on the labelled subset with repository-grouped cross-validation; it is not part of the primary comparison.)
- **5 Agentvow record**: static files/docs checks on every pull request; the full audit (`full_arm5.py`) on pull requests that change Python files and carry a tests, lint or impact claim; every other tests, lint or impact claim is UNKNOWN (cannot be run). Ranking score: CONTRADICTED 3 > attention 2 > UNKNOWN on a tests, lint or impact claim 1 > else 0, ties by seeded hash. Policy decisions by `shadow_policy_v1.json`.
- **6 CI + Agentvow**: union of the top 10% of arm 1 and the top 10% of arm 5.
- **4 LLM judge**: run after labelling on a seeded random 100 of the labelled pull requests (description and diff, suspicion 1 to 5); compared on that subset only.

## 4. Label sample
`python3 research/shadow2/sample.py --seed 2005743871635774842 --cap 220 --rest-rate 0.10` after the cheap arms and `full_arm5.py` have run and before any label exists. Flagged set: arm 5 strict policy review, or arm 3 in its top 15% of lexical scores, or arm 1 failing or pending. Inclusion probabilities and weights (1/probability) are recorded per pull request. First-pass labels by Sonnet agents with the audit-study prompt, blind to every arm's output; a seeded 20% second pass by Opus; same validation (`validate.py`).

## 5. Analysis (as implemented in `analyze.py`, 9 tests)
Class per labelled pull request: positive (a FALSE statement), negative (no FALSE, at least one TRUE), unscorable. Primary: **weighted recall of positives at a 20% review budget** per arm (top 20% of the pool by score), with the pairwise differences arm 5 minus arm 1 and arm 5 minus arm 3, and arm 6 minus arm 1, each with a repository cluster bootstrap (5,000 resamples, analysis seed). Unique catches: positives in arm 5's top set and in neither arm 1's nor arm 3's. Policy tables: review burden over the whole pool (census); weighted false-clear rate and review precision on labelled pull requests. Sensitivity: unscorable pull requests counted as positive and as negative. The seeded false-statement gate-strength test is run separately by a script written after this freeze, labelled by construction, and reported apart from the primary result.

## 6. Negative results, stated now
S2 not met: "the record adds nothing measurable over CI plus cheap text triage in this sample." Strict policy burden above 70%: reported as impractical. Arm 5 below arm 2 or arm 0 at the budget: reported as uninformative in this sample. Fewer than 20 positives: mechanics only.

## 7. Limits (as the design) and deviations log
Evidence-grounded LLM labels from one model family; low prevalence; Python-only checks cover a minority of pull requests; arm 5's non-Python cases are UNKNOWN by construction; temporal validity of heads and file lists; the pool is smaller than designed.
