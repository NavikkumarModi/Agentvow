| **ResearchEnvBench** (Wang et al., arXiv:2603.06739, 2026-03-06, revised 03-11) | environment synthesis for research repositories; success = executes at runtime; failures dominated by incomplete dependency resolution and version coupling. **The arXiv page names no peer-reviewed venue** (the write-up says IJCAI 2026: unconfirmed) | 44 repositories; five-stage verification including a self-report-versus-hidden-probe discrepancy criterion; best distributed-readiness 37.5%.
